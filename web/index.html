To install:

Connect your SMK directly to the computer by USB and power it on. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.

Close Studio and other MIDI apps, confirm the version, then use the installer below. Do not unplug USB or switch off while writing.

Choose your SMK-37 Pro or SMK-37 Elite. The installer automatically downloads the required official 016 file from a pinned mirror, checks its checksum, and prepares Felucca SMK in your browser. No file selection or upload is needed.

First installation or coming from stock firmware?

First installation

Your keyboard must be running official firmware 016. Choose the same Pro or Elite model, select First installation, then install over normal USB.

Updating Felucca SMK

Choose Update for public version 0.9.22 or later. Earlier private Test11–Test21 builds use a different updater and require a separate migration; this installer will stop before writing to them.

Meet your new custom SMK-37 controls...


Choose a group to highlight the controls and learn what they do.


Knobs
KNOB BANK off
Bank 1 matches the FM-1: knob 1 master volume, 2 tempo, 3 factory preset, and 4 track.
KNOB BANK on
Bank 2: knob 1 track, 2 synth engine, 3 factory preset, and 4 page.
KNOBS 5–8
Edit the four parameter cards on the current screen in either bank.
Turn clockwise
Increase a value or move forward through choices.
Sound pages
PATCH / HOME
Return to the mix view and preset browsing. The < and > buttons browse factory sounds here.
PARA / SAVE
Open Projects without saving immediately. Knob 5 selects a slot; > saves and < loads.
FX
Cycle through effects, extra effects, and performance controls.
GLOBE
Tap to cycle through global controls, controls and Projects. Hold to open grouped navigation; K5 selects the family, K6 selects a page, and > opens it.
ARP / REPEAT
Toggle the arpeggiator or note repeat and open its controls.
SCALE / CHORD
Toggle scale snapping or chord playing and open the corresponding page.
BT / EDIT
Tap to cycle the synth editing pages, including FM6 operator pages for FM6 tracks. Hold for Settings; K5 chooses a setting, K6 changes it, and > saves. Bluetooth is not enabled in this build.
Play & record
SEQ
Cycle through sequencer setup, step editing, step detail, and automation.
PLAY
Start or stop playback. On Song Chain, start the arranged sections.
REC
Toggle recording for the selected track. Recording starts playback when stopped.
PADS / < >
Use pads for step entry on the step pages; the arrow buttons change the editing position.
Keep a take
Recording stays in memory until you save a project.
Keys & wheels
37 KEYS
Play the selected track with velocity-sensitive piano keys.
OCT− / OCT+
Shift the keyboard down or up an octave, within the three-octave shift range.
PITCH
Bend the note pitch; the wheel returns to its center position.
MOD
Apply modulation to the selected sound.
SUSTAIN
Connect a sustain pedal to hold notes after releasing keys. 
Pads & faders
16 PADS
Play velocity-sensitive notes or enter steps when a step-editing page is open.
PAD BANK
Choose the pad bank in Settings to access the second set of pad notes.
FADER BANK off
The four faders control levels for tracks 1–4.
FADER BANK on
The four faders control attack, decay, sustain, and release for the selected track.
Illuminated buttons
Lights show active banks, playing modes, and transport state.
Save & arrange
Hold PATCH
Open the preset browser. K5 selects engine or USER, K6 browses, K7 selects ALL or FAV, and K8 adds or removes a favorite. > loads the selected sound.
Hold PARA
Open Undo/Redo: > swaps the last sound edit, < swaps the last pattern edit.
PROJECTS
Press PARA to open Projects. Knob 5 selects 1–4 (A–D in Studio); > saves and < loads.
USER PRESETS
With KNOB BANK on, knob 4 selects User Presets. Knob 5 chooses U01–U08; > stores and < loads.
SONG CHAIN
Use bank 2 knob 4 to reach Song Chain. > adds a section and < removes one. Save a project to keep it.
SEQ TOOLS
Use bank 2 knob 4 to reach Seq Tools. > runs the selected tool; < toggles its one-level undo/redo.
Before saving
Stop playback and recording, release keys and pedal, then wait for the saved confirmation.

From performance to saved project

  

    
Expressive playing
37 velocity-sensitive keys, sustain, pitch and modulation wheels, 16 pads with two banks, illuminated controls, and eight mapped encoders.

    
Thirteen sound engines
Explore the Felucca 1.5.1 engines and factory sounds, including six-operator FM. Shape each track with insert effects, delay, reverb, synced envelopes and LFOs.

    
Four-track sequencing
Record and edit patterns, add parameter locks, and vary steps with probability, ratchets and timing nudges. A metronome and count-in help you record.

    
Build a song
Arrange up to 16 sections. Each track can borrow a pattern from saved projects A–D or stay silent, with 1–16 repeats per section.

    
Shape a pattern
Reverse, shift, clear or generate a sequence, with one-level pattern undo/redo. Sound edits and preset loads have their own gesture-based undo/redo. Drum-lane tools let you work on one lane at a time.

    
Keep your sounds
Browse by engine or favorites, rename on the device, and sync stars with Studio. Eight device preset slots store your sounds, including FM6 patches. Four project slots keep sounds, patterns, song configuration and settings together.

    
Connected Studio
Edit the connected SMK over USB, manage sounds in Library, and load or save projects. Browser Studio and Simulator run the native SMK engine, sequencing and virtual storage on your computer.

    
Made for hands-on control
Bank 1 follows the FM-1: volume, tempo, presets, track, then four page parameters. Hold GLO for grouped navigation or BT for Settings. FM6 operator and envelope pages use upstream editing rules. The MIDI monitor shows events or sounding notes, including sustain. MIDI Learn and Program Change add external control.

    
Your look
Choose Stage Red, Matrix, Vintage DX7, Model D, Vaporwave and more for Studio. The device has 23 palettes and regular or bold text, including light and high-contrast choices.
